How it works

Quick Guide

Browse tabs: Each folder tab filters the cabinet to one cloak type.

Featured cloak: Each tab opens with its featured cloak selected.

Randomizers: Use Randomize All or Randomize This Tab to spin through cloaks and land on one result.

Popup details: Click any cloak card to open a small popup with the token info and the external item link.

Add your character: Send a new batch of token rows in the same format and the cabinet can be updated with another tab or refreshed data.
